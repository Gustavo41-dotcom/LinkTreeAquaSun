A Hybrid Solar System for Water Purification

The AquaSun project proposes a solution to water scarcity and energy inefficiency by integrating high-tech solar energy with active distillation.

How it Works & Impact

  • Solar Distillation: Converts unsafe water into safe drinking water using thermal energy.
  • Hybrid Integration: Integrates photovoltaic panels with Peltier modules for heat recovery.
  • High Efficiency: Designed to increase overall system yield compared to traditional methods.
  • ODS 6 & 7: Directly contributing to Clean Water and Affordable & Clean Energy.

Sketch - Prototype

Our prototype features a copper pipe system for heat exchange, Peltier modules for active distillation, and an aluminum heat sink for condensation.
