The Water & Energy Crisis

Isolated and off-grid communities face severe shortages of safe drinking water. Concurrently, solar panels operating in hot climates lose massive energy efficiency due to extreme thermal overheating.

Hybrid Solar Distillation

AquaSun captures the panel's wasted thermal energy to heat and purify contaminated or brackish water through thermodynamic distillation driven by Peltier modules.

Dual Social Return

By cooling the photovoltaic hardware, we boost electricity output while generating 100% pure distilled water without mechanical filters, complex chemicals, or heavy maintenance costs.
